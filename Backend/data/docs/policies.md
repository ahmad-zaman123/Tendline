# Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $25. Orders ship within 2 business days of being placed.

# Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping is free for damaged or faulty items. For other returns, the customer pays return shipping.

# Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return being received. Refunds must be approved by the support team.

# Warranty
Desks and chairs have a 5-year warranty covering manufacturing faults. Accessories have a 1-year warranty. Normal wear and accidental damage are not covered.

# Recalled parts
Desk frames with part number NW-4471 were recalled in September 2026 because of a faulty height lock. Stop adjusting the desk and contact support for a free replacement.

# Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and PayPal. We do not offer payment plans.
